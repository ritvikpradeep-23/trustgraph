Welcome to TrustGraph

Not sure about a message? Check it in one click. TrustGraph flags signs of a scam and explains why. It only warns, and never hides or deletes anything.

  1. 1

    Start the TrustGraph server, or use the Basic check

    For the full analysis, run the TrustGraph server on this computer (by default at http://127.0.0.1:8000). If it isn't running, TrustGraph still works with a simpler Basic check that runs on your device. Results say which one was used.

    Checking for the server…

  2. 2

    Open a supported site

    WhatsApp Web, Gmail, Facebook Messenger (facebook.com/messages), or Instagram DMs. Gmail, Messenger, and Instagram support is experimental.

  3. 3

    Scan the chat, or check one message

    Click the round TrustGraph button at the right edge of the chat to check the conversation you have open. A side panel shows the result without covering your messages. To check a single message, hover it and click the small shield. On any other site, select the text, right-click, and choose Check with TrustGraph.

Try it

Sample checks aren't added to your counts.

Your privacy, in short

  • Nothing is read until you click: the TrustGraph button reads the messages of the open chat; the shield or right-click reads one message.
  • What you check is sent only to the TrustGraph server you set (your own computer by default). Your own messages aren't sent.
  • No message text is stored. TrustGraph keeps only your settings and daily counts.
  • A message is shared with the scam database only if you click Report as scam and confirm.
  • No analytics, no ads, and no selling or sharing of data.
